Weather is a minimalist, lightweight, and ad-free weather application for Android featuring a native home screen widget.

Features:
• Direct Open-Meteo API Connection: Fast weather data retrieval with zero telemetry, no tracking, and no proprietary API keys.
• Accurate Weather Metrics: Real-time temperature, apparent temperature, relative humidity, wind speed, and atmospheric surface pressure.
• Solar PV Forecast: Physically modeled rooftop and balcony photovoltaic output estimation (based on a 5 kWp reference system).
• 24-Hour and 7-Day Forecast: Clean overview of upcoming hourly conditions and multi-day trends.
• Interactive Home Screen Widget: Live system clock (TextClock), live weather conditions, min/max temperatures, and solar PV status on your home screen.
• Configurable Auto-Refresh: Choose your preferred update interval in settings (15 minutes, 30 minutes, 1 hour, or manual only).
• In-App Legal & Privacy Notice: Fully native, offline-readable Impressum (§ 5 DDG), Privacy Policy (GDPR/TDDDG), and contact information directly in the app.
• Global Location Search: Worldwide geocoding via Open-Meteo with local persistence.
• Unit Switching: Support for Celsius (°C), Fahrenheit (°F), and Kelvin (K).
• 100% Free & Open Source: Licensed under the GNU General Public License v3.0.
