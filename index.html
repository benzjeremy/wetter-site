Completely private and tracking-free: accessible as an interactive Canvas web app and a lightweight native Android app with home screen widget.

Physikalisches Fundament Physical Foundation

Das Photovoltaik-Ertragsmodell The Photovoltaic Calculation Model

Transparente Modellierung: Keine geschätzten Phantasiewerte, sondern nachvollziehbare Halbleiterphysik. Transparent modeling: Grounded in semiconductor physics rather than arbitrary heuristics.

P_pv = P_peak × (G_eff / 1000 W/m²) × [1 + γ × (T_cell - 25°C)] × η_inverter
P_peak = 5000 W (5 kWp): Nennleistung unter Standard-Testbedingungen (1000 W/m², 25°C).Peak rated capacity under Standard Test Conditions (1000 W/m², 25°C).
G_eff = G_clearsky × (1 - 0.75 × (bewölkung / 100)^3.4): Effektive solare Einstrahlung nach Bewölkungsdämpfung.Effective solar irradiance after cloud attenuation.
γ = -0.0038 / °C: Temperaturkoeffizient monokristalliner Siliziumzellen (-0,38%/°C Leistungsabfall bei Erwärmung).Temperature power coefficient for monocrystalline silicon (-0.38%/°C).
η_inverter = 0.96 (96%): Wirkungsgrad moderner transformatorloser Wechselrichter.Conversion efficiency of modern solar string inverters.
